# 🚀 Cevora

> AI-powered placement intelligence platform for college students.

---

## 📖 About

Cevora is an intelligence-first platform designed to simplify campus placements.

Students often struggle with scattered information across WhatsApp groups, Telegram channels, Google Drive folders, seniors, and placement notices. Cevora brings everything together into one intelligent platform to help students prepare smarter.

---

## ✨ Vision

Build the most trusted platform for placement preparation by combining:

- 📅 Company placement timelines
- 💼 Interview experiences
- 📝 Online assessment archives
- 🤖 AI-powered career assistance
- 📊 Personalized insights
- 🎯 Placement roadmaps

---

## 🎯 Current Status

🚧 This project is currently under active development.

The MVP is being built as part of a hackathon and will continue evolving into a full-scale product.
